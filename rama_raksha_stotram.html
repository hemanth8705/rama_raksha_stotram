How to use this page

The original Sanskrit verse is always visible.

Use Simple Meaning when you want to quickly understand what the verse is saying.

Use Understanding the Verse + Word-by-Word when you want to study the verse in greater detail.

The detailed explanation follows this order: Understanding the Verse → Word-by-Word Meaning.

The Sanskrit text and the variants supplied in the original text, such as భాలం/ఫాలం and గృణామి/గృహ్ణామి, are retained.
